Settings & backups

Control your budget and protect your records.

💱 Budget & exchange rate

Enter your own TWD → MYR conversion rate. The tracker does not fetch live exchange rates.

Example: 0.135 = NT$ 100 ≈ RM 13.50

💾 Back up your travel memories

Records are saved automatically only in this browser on this device. Your iPhone and your partner's Android maintain separate copies. Back up regularly!

📤 Restore or combine records

Choose a JSON backup. Merge adds missing entries without deleting existing ones. Replace overwrites all data on this device.

🔐 Private by design: No account, no ads, no cloud database. GitHub Pages hosts this page, not your spending data. Clearing browser website data can erase your records.

🖨️ Trip summary & data safety

Print a clean trip spending summary, or select “Save to PDF” using your phone's or computer's print options. Transfers and EasyCard top-ups are excluded from spending totals.

Tip for sharing expenses across phones without live sync: export a JSON backup from one phone, send it to the other, then select Merge. Repeating the same backup won't double-count existing entries. If both phones independently record the same purchase, it must be removed manually.
